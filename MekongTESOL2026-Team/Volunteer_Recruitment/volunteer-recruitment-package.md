**🔷 VOLUNTEER POSITIONS AVAILABLE**

**1. Logistics Supporters**

Assist with attendee check-in, distribute conference materials, and provide information to participants upon arrival.

**3. Hospitality and Information Desk**
Welcome and assist participants, provide directions, answer inquiries about the conference schedule, venue, and local attractions.

**4. Event Photographers**
Capture memorable moments throughout the conference — sessions, networking events, and key activities — to document the event and create visual content.

- Volunteers must be residents of Can Tho City (temporarily or permanently)

## 3. GRAPHIC DESIGN BRIEF — VOLUNTEER RECRUITMENT POSTER

**Event:** 5th Mekong TESOL International Conference — Volunteers Needed
**Style:** Dark Indigo / Gold — Professional Conference Aesthetic
**Usage:** Facebook post, Story, Print (A3)

---

### 3.1 Overview & Mood

The visual identity should communicate **prestige, internationalism, and energy**. Shift from the playful teal/mint gradient of the 4th edition to a richer, more authoritative palette that befits a growing international conference while still feeling welcoming and youthful enough to attract student volunteers.

**Mood keywords:** Sophisticated · Energetic · Inclusive · Professional · International

---

### 3.2 Color Palette

| Role | Color | Hex |
|------|-------|-----|
| Primary Background | Deep Navy Indigo | `#0D0D2B` or `#1A1040` |
| Secondary Background | Dark Purple | `#2D1B69` |
| Accent / Highlight | Gold / Amber | `#F5C518` or `#E8A020` |
| Accent 2 | Soft Lavender | `#A78BFA` |
| Text (Primary) | White | `#FFFFFF` |
| Text (Secondary) | Light Gold | `#F0D080` |
| Card / Section Backgrounds | Semi-transparent white | `rgba(255,255,255,0.08)` |

**Gradient direction:** Top-left deep navy → Bottom-right dark purple (diagonal, 135°)

---

### 3.3 Typography

| Element | Font Suggestion | Style |
|---------|----------------|-------|
| "VOLUNTEERS NEEDED" headline | Montserrat ExtraBold / Poppins Black | All caps, very large (60–80pt) |
| Conference name | Montserrat Bold | Title case, 18–22pt |
| Section labels (POSITIONS, REQUIREMENTS) | Montserrat SemiBold | All caps, inside gold pill/badge shape |
| Body text | Montserrat Regular or Inter | 11–13pt, white |
| Dates / Important info | Poppins Bold | Highlighted in gold badge |

> ⚠️ Avoid serif fonts. Keep the hierarchy clean: 1 display font + 1 body font maximum.

### 3.5 Graphic Elements & Details

**Headline Treatment:**
- "VOLUNTEERS" in large white bold text
- "NEEDED" in the same size, with **gold gradient fill** or outlined in gold
- Subtle gold sparkle/star accents around the headline (≤ 4 stars, avoid clutter)

**Section Badges:**
- Replace the teal rounded-rectangle badges from v4 with **gold-bordered pill shapes** on dark semi-transparent background
- Section label text: white or light gold, all caps, bold

**Illustration Style:**
- Flat vector illustration of a diverse group of young people (similar energy to v4)
- Color-treat illustration to match the palette: indigo/purple tones for clothing, gold accents
- OR: Use a decorative abstract geometric pattern (overlapping circles, hexagons in gold/lavender) as the hero element if no illustration is available

**Date Badges:**
- Solid gold (`#F5C518`) rounded rectangles with dark navy text — high contrast, eye-catching
- Deadline badge: `28 JUNE 2026`
- Conference badge: `19 JULY 2026`

**QR Code:**
- White QR code on dark background, or dark QR code in a white rounded square card
- Update QR code once application link is confirmed

**Footer / Logos:**
- Place partner logos (Gia Viet, University of Wollongong, CTUMP) on a slightly lighter dark strip at the bottom
- Logos should be white/monochrome versions if available for consistency

---

### 3.6 Canva Tips (if using Canva)

- Start with a dark background template (search "conference poster dark")
- Use **Montserrat** or **Poppins** — both available free on Canva
- Use Canva's "Brand Kit" to lock in the hex codes above
- For the gold gradient text on headline: use Canva Pro's "gradient text" effect, or place a gold rectangle behind white-outlined text
- Recommended Canva template tag to search: **"event volunteer dark blue gold"**
